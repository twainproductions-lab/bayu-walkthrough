# Bayu Ballroom walkthrough

Interactive 3D walkthrough of the Bayu Ballroom, JW Marriott Kuala Lumpur, with the wedding layout for June & Co.

Live page: https://twainproductions-lab.github.io/bayu-walkthrough/

## Photoreal 360° tour

Romina & Aidan, 2 January 2027. Eight rendered viewpoints you can look around and move between, in dinner and house lighting, plus a gallery of stills. Rendered in Blender Cycles at 8192 × 4096 and served at 4096 × 2048 so it opens on phones.

Tour: https://twainproductions-lab.github.io/bayu-walkthrough/tour/

The décor follows the Fleurs at Marrakesh proposal (rev 01) and is indicative only; the real set-up may vary.
