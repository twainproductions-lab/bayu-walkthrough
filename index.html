Spacing check

Measured from the chair backs. The layout keeps at least 0.6 m between seated guests so a server can pass, and 0.9 m or more where people walk.

104 × 49 ft (31.7 × 14.9 m), stage 6 ft deep with a 12 ft run to the LED wall, screen 15 ft × 9 ft 11 in, from the hotel plan. Stage height (about 1 m) and ceiling height (about 6 m) are estimated from the photos and should be confirmed with the hotel.

Dance floor 30 × 18 ft (540 sq ft) with the couple’s table on it, front left. The hotel’s AV table stays at the stage’s front-left corner. Buffets: two 16 ft lines of six chafing dishes, one each side of the entrance.
